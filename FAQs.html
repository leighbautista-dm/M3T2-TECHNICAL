Are these socks clean?

Yes, but they have experienced things. They possess the quiet wisdom of garments that have survived high-temperature sanitization cycles and aggressive folding techniques.

Can I wear them to a job interview?

Only if the job involves disrupting an industry, starting a cult, or confusing middle management. For formal finance roles, wear them under long trousers and channel your inner anarchy silently.

What if I lose one in the wash?

Congratulations, you now have three individual socks that can be rotated into an entirely new triangular pairing system. You are practically practicing quantum physics at this point.

Do you sell single socks?

All of our socks are single by choice. They don't need a partner to validate their self-worth.
