Our Footprint

Who We Are

We are a collective of former corporate accountants, disillusioned baristas, and professional daydreamers who looked down at our feet one Tuesday morning and realized we were wearing two entirely different pairs of socks. Instead of changing, we decided to change the world.

Our Philosophy

Life is too short to spend matching socks like a human spreadsheet. We believe in chaos, comfort, and the radical notion that nobody is looking at your ankles anyway—and if they are, they better be impressed.

The Solitude Pledge

  • 100% Mismatched Guarantee: If you somehow receive two matching socks, we will personally send you an apology note written by a cat walking across a keyboard.
  • Ethically Sourced Yarn: Harvested exclusively from sheep who are enthusiastic about modern art.
  • Zero Judgment: Your feet, your rules.
